## condensed

I've kept this private for years. The privacy cost me more than it protected me.

My father had bipolar disorder. In 2022 I caught myself doing the things I remembered him doing, and I was diagnosed that December. Since then: the highs I miss, the crashes, the people I hurt and lost, a long run of medications, ketamine, TMS, three medical leaves from work, and doctors who now question the label itself.

I'm sharing it for the people I've hurt along the way, and for anyone reading this at 2 a.m. who recognizes themselves in it.
