This policy covers the personal tools I build and run for myself that sign in to Google, such as my private
writing archive and command-line utilities. They are registered with Google as the OAuth app "gogcli".

**Who uses them.** Only me, with my own Google account. The tools are not offered to anyone else.

**What they access.** Read-only access to my own Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Tasks and YouTube data, plus my
account's email address. They never send mail, change files or modify anything in the account.

**What happens to the data.** It is copied into my private archive (a private repository and private cloud
storage that only I can reach) so I can search and reread my own writing. It is not sold, shared, published or
used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI models.

**Google API Services.** The tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

**Removing access.** Access can be revoked at any time at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

**Contact.** Questions about this policy: reach me through any of the profiles on the [About](/about/) page.

Last updated: 16 November 2026.
