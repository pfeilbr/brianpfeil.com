A data-driven PWA for narrowing a college list down to one school. Each campus gets a full visit guide; a financial-aid estimator handles the awkward cases (including divorced-parent income); a side-by-side comparison table puts the numbers together; and a Decision Board turns tour-day ratings into a ranked shortlist.
